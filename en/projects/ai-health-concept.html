AI Health Management Product Concept

A competition-oriented product concept that combines personal health records, guided moxibustion workflows, AI-assisted recommendations, mobile interaction and multi-device collaboration.

Overview

Product opportunity

Home health-management experiences are often fragmented across instructions, records and disconnected devices. This concept explores a clearer service flow that connects user profiles, guided sessions, reminders, educational content and device status while preserving a strict non-diagnostic boundary.

My Role

What I contributed

  • User-problem framing and target-scenario definition
  • Feature map and service-flow planning
  • Mobile interaction and information hierarchy
  • AI recommendation concept and safety wording
  • Moxibustion session and reminder workflow
  • Multi-device collaboration concept
  • Competition materials and team coordination
  • Risk, privacy and evidence-boundary review

The work demonstrates product thinking and cross-domain integration. It does not claim clinical efficacy, diagnosis capability or completed hardware validation.

Limitations and Safety

Non-clinical boundary

This project is a product concept. It must not replace professional diagnosis, treatment or emergency services. Any future implementation would require clinical, regulatory, privacy, accessibility and hardware-safety review.
