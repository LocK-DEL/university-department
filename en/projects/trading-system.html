MARKET ANALYSIS · RISK SYSTEM

Multi-Timeframe Market Analysis and Risk Reporting System

A Python-based workflow that collects public market data, separates 15-minute, 1-hour and 4-hour analysis responsibilities, produces conditional scenarios with invalidation rules, calculates position and maximum-risk examples, and exports reviewable reports.

Overview

Why this project exists

Manual market analysis can become inconsistent when timeframes, conditions and risk assumptions are mixed together. This project turns those decisions into explicit modules and reports so the process can be inspected and reviewed. It is an engineering and risk-communication project, not a claim of predictive accuracy.

Problem

Problems addressed

01

Mixed timeframe roles

Separates lower, middle and higher timeframe responsibilities instead of blending signals.

02

Direction without conditions

Each scenario includes trigger and invalidation conditions rather than a one-line prediction.

03

Missing risk calculations

Calculates example position size, margin and maximum loss from defined inputs.

04

Poor reviewability

Exports structured Markdown reports so assumptions remain visible after the analysis.

Core Features

Processes 15-minute, 1-hour and 4-hour data as separate analytical layers.

03

Conditional scenarios

Produces bullish and bearish scenarios with explicit trigger and invalidation logic.

04

Risk calculations

Shows how capital, stop distance and risk limits change position sizing.

Execution guard

Experiments with rule-based checks before a plan can move to an execution stage.

07

Markdown output

Exports market state, conditions and risk assumptions into a reviewable report.

08

Uses fixed fixtures and mocked responses to test analysis and reporting modules.
