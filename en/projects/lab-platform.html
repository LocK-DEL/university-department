Overview

Why this project exists

Research groups often spread team profiles, publications, study materials and recruitment information across disconnected documents and messaging channels. The platform explores how one responsive website can provide a clearer public entry point while keeping content maintainable.

My Role

What I owned

  • Information architecture and navigation planning
  • Reusable React component design
  • Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile
  • Content structure for team, outputs and research areas
  • Study and recruitment page organization
  • Static export and deployment configuration
  • Visual consistency and interaction review
  • Accessibility and link validation

The project demonstrates frontend product delivery and research-content organization. It does not expose private laboratory records, internal account systems or confidential research data.
